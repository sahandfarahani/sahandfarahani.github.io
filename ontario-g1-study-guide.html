How to Study for the Ontario G1 Test

A practical guide to learning road signs, understanding driving rules and preparing for your G1 knowledge test.

1. Start with the Official MTO Driver's Handbook

The best starting point is the Official MTO Driver's Handbook . It covers the traffic signs, traffic lights, driving rules and safe driving practices you need to learn.

Read a section, summarize the main points in your own words, and then test yourself without looking at the answers.

2. Study These Important Topics

  • Traffic signs, signals and pavement markings
  • Right-of-way and rules at intersections
  • Speed limits and safe driving practices
  • Sharing the road with pedestrians, cyclists and other vehicles
  • School buses and emergency vehicles
  • Passing other vehicles and changing lanes
  • Seatbelts, headlights and collision reporting

Use the handbook as your primary reference, and revisit any topic you find difficult.

3. Learn to Recognize Road Signs

Pay attention to each sign's shape, colour and symbol. These features help you distinguish regulatory signs, warning signs and signs providing information.

For example, an eight-sided red STOP sign requires a complete stop. A triangular YIELD sign means you must let traffic in or near the intersection go first, stopping if necessary.

Review the handbook's traffic signs reference regularly.

4. Test Your Knowledge: 10 G1 Practice Questions

Test yourself on common Ontario driving rules. Choose your answer mentally before opening each question to reveal the answer and explanation.

Question 1: What should you do when a traffic light turns yellow?

Answer: Stop if you can do so safely; otherwise, proceed with caution.

Explanation: A yellow light warns that the red light is about to appear. Do not speed up to beat the light.

Question 2: What must you do at a flashing red traffic light?

Answer: Come to a complete stop and proceed only when it is safe.

Explanation: A flashing red light requires a complete stop, not merely slowing down.

Question 3: You have a green light and want to turn left. Who must you yield to?

Answer: Oncoming traffic and pedestrians crossing in your path.

Explanation: A green light permits movement when the way is clear. It does not give you priority over oncoming traffic or pedestrians.

Question 4: At an uncontrolled intersection, two vehicles arrive at the same time from different roads. Who has the right-of-way?

Answer: The vehicle approaching from the right.

Explanation: At an intersection without signs or traffic lights, if both vehicles arrive at the same time, the driver on the left must yield to the driver on the right.

Question 5: Where should you stop at a stop sign if there is a marked stop line?

Answer: At the stop line.

Explanation: You must come to a complete stop. When a stop line is marked, stop at that line before proceeding when the way is clear.

Question 6: A school bus ahead of you has its red lights flashing. How far back must you stop?

Answer: At least 20 metres behind the bus.

Explanation: When approaching from behind, stop at least 20 metres away. Remain stopped until the bus moves or its red lights stop flashing and its stop arm is no longer activated.

Question 7: A school bus has its red lights flashing on a road divided by a physical median. Must traffic approaching from the opposite direction stop?

Answer: No, not when the physical median separates the opposing traffic.

Explanation: On a road with a median strip, vehicles approaching from behind the bus must stop; traffic travelling in the opposite direction is not required to stop for that bus.

Question 8: What must you do when a pedestrian is crossing at a pedestrian crossover?

Answer: Yield and wait until the pedestrian has completely crossed safely.

Explanation: Drivers must yield to pedestrians at pedestrian crossovers. Do not proceed while pedestrians are still crossing.

Question 9: You are leaving a private driveway to enter a road. Who has the right-of-way?

Answer: Traffic already on the road and pedestrians on the sidewalk.

Explanation: You must yield to vehicles on the road and pedestrians using the sidewalk before entering.

Question 10: You approach a yield sign. What should you do?

Answer: Slow down or stop if necessary, and yield until it is safe to proceed.

Explanation: You must give the right-of-way to traffic in the intersection or on the intersecting road. A yield sign does not mean you can continue without checking.

How did you do? Review any answers you missed in the Official MTO Driver's Handbook . These are study examples, not actual or guaranteed G1 exam questions.

5. Follow a Simple Study Plan

  1. Session 1: Read about traffic signs and signals.
  2. Session 2: Review right-of-way and intersection rules.
  3. Session 3: Study safe driving and sharing the road.
  4. Session 4: Practice questions and review mistakes.
  5. Session 5: Revisit weak areas and test yourself again.
